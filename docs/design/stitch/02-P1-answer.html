04.
Short Essay

Briefly explain the concept of 'cognitive dissonance' and provide one practical example of how it might manifest in a consumer purchasing decision.

Cognitive dissonance is when a person feels confused because they have too many choices. For example, when buying a car, a consumer might not know which brand is best and feels stressed about picking the wrong one, so they just buy what their friend has.

warning CORRECTION REQUIRED
Partial Credit (1/3)

Expected Key Points:
1. Discomfort arising from holding conflicting beliefs/attitudes or behavior contradicting beliefs.
2. Post-purchase rationalization (e.g., buyer's remorse after expensive purchase).

Evaluator Note: Student conflated 'choice overload' (paradox of choice) with cognitive dissonance.
